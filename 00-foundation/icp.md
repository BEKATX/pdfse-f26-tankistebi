# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Davit, 20, 3rd-year CS student living on the 3rd floor of Block F at KIU.

**Their situation:** Takes 5 courses, lives on the 3rd floor of Block F, does laundry 1–2 times a week (usually late on Sunday or Monday nights). He has to carry a heavy laundry basket down 4 flights of stairs to the -1 floor laundry room. *(Evidence: Log 01, Log 02)*

**The moment the problem shows up:** Sunday evening around 21:00, when he carries his basket down to the -1 floor, only to find all 4 machines running with 40+ minutes left on the display. *(Evidence: Log 01, Log 06)*

**What it costs them:** 
- 30–45 minutes wasted per empty trip walking up and down stairs between floor 3 and -1. *(Evidence: Log 01, Log 02)*
- Having wet clothes dumped onto dusty chairs or tables when delayed. *(Evidence: Log 01, Log 05)*
- Clothes sitting forgotten in stopped machines for over an hour because the -1 floor is out of sight. *(Evidence: Log 03, Log 06)*

**What they do about it today:** 
- Walks back up to his room and sets a phone timer (which drifts by 10–15 minutes compared to the machine). *(Evidence: Log 02, Log 05)*
- Leaves his laundry basket on the -1 floor right in front of a washer to hold an informal spot in line. *(Evidence: Log 06)*
- Asks in the dorm Messenger chat ("is machine 3 free?"), which gets ignored. *(Evidence: Log 01, Log 03, Log 06)*

**What they are NOT:**
- Students living in off-campus apartments in Kutaisi with their own private washing machines.
- Students who go home to Tbilisi every single weekend and take their laundry with them.
