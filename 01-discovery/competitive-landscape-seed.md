## The strongest incumbent

**Walking down to the -1 floor manually.** It appears in every single log (01–06). While physically exhausting (hauling heavy baskets down 3–4 flights of stairs), residents trust their own eyes over chat groups or phone timers. Anything we build has to beat the physical trip before the student picks up their basket.

## Alternatives nobody mentioned

- **Dedicated campus laundry apps:** Zero students named any software or automated tracking platform for dorm laundry.
- **Paper sign-up sheets on the door:** Nobody uses physical reservation schedules on the -1 floor.
