## The script you read from

1. Walk me through the last time you did laundry, from grabbing your basket to putting it on the drying rack.
2. Tell me about the last time you went down to the -1 floor and all machines were occupied. How did you handle it?
3. Have you ever had someone take your clothes out of a machine, or had to take someone else's out? Where were the clothes placed?
4. How long is it acceptable for finished clothes to sit in a machine before an incoming student can move them?
5. How do you track when your cycle finishes? Have you ever noticed your phone timer drifting from the machine's actual display?
6. Have you ever forgotten your clothes on the -1 floor, or seen people leave baskets on the floor to claim a spot in line?
7. Who else in your block has strong opinions on the laundry room that we should talk to?
