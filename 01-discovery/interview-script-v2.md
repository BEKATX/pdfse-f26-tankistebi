## The script you will read from (for Interviews 05 and 06)

1. Walk me through the last time you did laundry, from grabbing your basket to putting it in the dryer or drying rack.
2. Tell me about the last time you went down and all machines were occupied. How did you handle it?
3. Have you ever had someone take your clothes out of a machine, or had to take someone else's out? Walk me through what happened and where the clothes were placed.
4. How long do you consider it acceptable for finished clothes to sit in an idle machine before an incoming student is allowed to move them?
5. How do you track when your cycle finishes? Have you ever noticed your phone timer drifting from the machine's actual display?
6. If a simple digital tracker existed where students scan once when starting a wash, would you trust other residents to keep it accurate?
7. Who else on your floor has strong feelings about the laundry situation that we should talk to?
