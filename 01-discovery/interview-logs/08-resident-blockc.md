# Interview 08 · Resident, Block G 2nd Floor · (code name: Resident-G203)

**Week:** 4 · **Asker:** GU · **Logger:** BT · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording  
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 2nd-year Management student living in Block G, washes clothes once every 7–10 days. We investigated why clothes are abandoned on -1 and how residents react to peer unloading nudges.  
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students are defensive about receiving a digital nudge to clear their laundry or prefer having strangers move their wet clothes.

**Workaround:** Relies on alarm clocks, which frequently ring while he is stuck in class or studying outside the dorm.  
**Cost:** Smelly clothes requiring a re-wash (wasting detergent and another cycle); blocked machine for fellow dorm mates.  
**Frequency:** Forgets finished laundry in machine approximately once a month.  
**Prediction check:** Confirmed. The student expressed guilt about blocking machines and strongly welcomed reminders via channels he already uses (Messenger).  
**Surprise ★:** Resistance to installing native mobile apps is very high; students strongly prefer a lightweight web app or Telegram bot over an app store download.  
**Commitment:** Connected his Messenger account to our test notification channel for pilot alerts.  
**Next:** In Interview 9, run a paper/clickable mockup feedback session on machine availability, timers, and the report button.
