# Interview 07 · Resident, Block F 1st Floor · (code name: Resident-F105)

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** BT · **Logger:** AK · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording  
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 3rd-year CS student living on the 1st floor of Block F, does laundry strictly during Sunday peak hours (20:00–23:00). We tested resident experiences with the informal "basket on the floor" queue system discovered in Interview 06.  
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students think physical basket queues on floor -1 are fair and function without friction.

**Quotes** (verbatim Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "კვირა საღამოს სამრეცხაოში რომ ჩადიხარ, სამი კალათი უკვე დევს რიგში იატაკზე. ვერ გაიგებ ვისია და ვინ ვის მერეა." / "When you go down to the laundry on Sunday evening, three baskets are already sitting in line on the floor. You can't tell whose is whose or who is after whom."
- "ერთხელ ჩემი კალათი ვიღაცამ გვერდზე გადადგა და თავისი სარეცხი ჩაყარა, მერე დერეფანში მოგვივიდა კამათი." / "Once someone pushed my basket aside and put their own laundry in, then we ended up having an argument in the hallway." ★
- "ციფრული რიგი რომ იყოს, სადაც ჩაეწერები და ტელეფონზე მოგივა 'შენი რიგია 10 წუთში', იატაკზე ნივთების დაყრა აღარავის დასჭირდება." / "If there were a digital queue where you queue up and your phone alerts 'your turn in 10 mins', nobody would need to dump things on the floor."
- "პირველ სართულზე ვცხოვრობ და მაინც მეზარება -1-ზე ჩასვლა იმის სანახავად, რომ რიგია." / "I live on the 1st floor and even for me it's annoying to walk down to -1 just to find out there is a line."

**Workaround:** Puts sticky notes with his room number on top of his basket to claim an in-person queue spot.  
**Cost:** 45–60 minutes spent monitoring the basement hallway to prevent line jumpers; social friction with neighbors.  
**Frequency:** Happens almost every Sunday evening.  
**Prediction check:** Confirmed. Physical basket queues cause disputes and confusion; students strongly prefer a transparent digital queue over anonymous baskets on the floor.  
**Surprise ★:** Students have started labeling baskets with room numbers and notes, proving they are trying to hack together an ownership/queue system on their own.  
**Commitment:** Agreed to test an experimental digital queue feature during Sunday evening rush.  
**Next:** In Interview 09, explore forgotten laundry in machines and whether residents welcome automated SMS/Messenger nudges.
