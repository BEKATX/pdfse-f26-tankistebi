# Interview 06 · Resident, Block F 3rd Floor · (code name: Resident-F304)

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** AK · **Logger:** BT · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording  
**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 3rd-year CS student living on the 3rd floor of Block F, does laundry on Sunday or Monday nights. We investigated the physical friction of traveling to the -1 floor and what happens when laundry is left in the basement.

**Workaround:** Either leaves the laundry basket on the floor at -1 as an informal reservation, or walks back up to wait in the room while checking the Messenger chat.
**Cost:** 40+ minutes of wasted walking and waiting; clothes left wet in a stopped machine for 2 hours; disputes over who was next in line.
**Frequency:** Happens almost every Sunday evening between 20:00 and 23:00.
**Prediction check:** Confirmed. Shows that physical distance to the -1 floor causes people to abandon clothes for hours and creates messy manual queue workarounds (leaving baskets on the floor).
**Surprise ★:** Leaving baskets on the floor at -1 is an active physical workaround students invented to hold queue spots, showing that line management and queue transparency are major pain points.
**Commitment:** Agreed to let us show him our synthesis patterns and follow up for testing.
